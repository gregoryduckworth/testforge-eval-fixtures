# Test strategy: CART-31 Checkout misprices carts: no bulk discount at 10 items, and SAVE5 can go negative

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-31 reports two mispricings in cartTotal (src/cart.js). Reading the code: bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount (report 1 reproduces and is kept as an expected failure). The SAVE5 report doesn't reproduce: goods are already floored at 0 and $4.99 shipping is added, so a $3 cart with SAVE5 costs 4.99. The tests reproduce report 1, pin the report-2 behaviour as a regression test, and check the boundaries on both sides.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal bulk discount at, below and above BULK_THRESHOLD (single and multi-line carts)
- cartTotal with SAVE5 when the coupon exceeds, equals or is below the goods value
- refusal of an unknown coupon

**Out of scope**
- Fixing src/cart.js (the ticket asks for reproduction before a fix)
