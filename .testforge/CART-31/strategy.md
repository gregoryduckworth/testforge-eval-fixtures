# Test strategy: CART-31 Checkout misprices carts: no bulk discount at 10 items, and SAVE5 can go negative

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-31 reports two cartTotal mispricings in src/cart.js. (1) bulkDiscount uses `itemCount(items) > BULK_THRESHOLD` (src/cart.js:22) though BULK_THRESHOLD's doc says "at least this many", so 10 items at $10 is charged $100 instead of $90. (2) A $3 cart with SAVE5 should cost $4.99; reading the code, cartTotal already clamps goods with Math.max(0, …) (line 31), so this symptom is expected not to reproduce — the test will assert the ticket's $4.99 and report whatever it shows.
