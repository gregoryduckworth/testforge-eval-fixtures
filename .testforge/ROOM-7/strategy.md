# Test strategy: ROOM-7 Book meeting rooms

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

createBookings() validates room/owner/start/length, refuses overlapping bookings per room, lists a room's bookings by start, and lets only the owner cancel. Unit tests with Vitest against the real module and its memoryStore prove each rule and its refusal, including a concurrent double-booking check (the clash check and the add are separate awaits).

## Scope

**In scope**
- book() validation and BookingError messages
- overlap rules incl. concurrent booking
- list() order and room filtering
- cancel() ownership and freeing time

**Out of scope**
- the app's database-backed BookingStore (not in the repo)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Race: two concurrent overlapping bookings both succeed because the clash check and add are separate async steps | high | TC10 books concurrently and expects exactly one success |
| Off-by-one at boundaries (30/240 minutes, back-to-back) | medium | TC5, TC6, TC9 |
