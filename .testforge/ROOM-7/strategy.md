# Test strategy: ROOM-7 Book meeting rooms

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

createBookings() validates a booking request (room, owner, half-hour UTC start, 30–240 min length in 30 min steps), refuses overlaps within a room, lists a room's bookings by start time and lets only the owner cancel. Unit tests with Vitest against memoryStore() prove each rule and its refusal, with the exact BookingError message.

## Scope

**In scope**
- book(), list() and cancel() of createBookings() with memoryStore()

**Out of scope**
- The app's database BookingStore (not in the repo)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|

| Cancel by someone else silently deletes a booking | high | TC13 checks the refusal and that the booking is still listed |
