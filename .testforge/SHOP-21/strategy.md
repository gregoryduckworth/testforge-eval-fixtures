# Test strategy: SHOP-21 Add automated accessibility checks to the e2e tests

Revision 2 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The shop serves four pages (/, /cart, /about, /contact, as listed in server.js and /sitemap.xml). This adds @axe-core/playwright scans (already a devDependency) to the Playwright e2e suite. Pages with no critical/serious WCAG 2.x A/AA violations get a passing scan. Pages that have them (/cart: images with no alt; /about: #c7c7c7 small print) keep the same assertion, marked test.fail and recorded as defects. Revision 2: each of those pages also gets a passing test that pins its known violations exactly, so a page that stops loading, or a new violation, fails the suite instead of being absorbed by test.fail. A sitemap check makes sure a new page can't go unscanned.

## Scope

**In scope**
- axe scan of each of the four pages on load
- axe scan of the contact page after sending a message (DOM changes: status message)
- pinning the exact known violations on the failing pages
- check that the scanned pages are exactly the pages the sitemap lists

**Out of scope**
- Fixing the violations in public/*.html (application source)
- Manual keyboard / screen-reader checks (axe cannot prove them)

## Environment

The Playwright webServer starts node server.js on 4174 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4174). Chromium is needed.

## Assumptions

- The real server (node server.js) runs for every test via the existing playwright webServer; nothing is faked.
- Blocking = axe impact critical or serious under tags wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa; moderate/minor violations are attached to the test report, not failed on.
- 'Report the violations on the others' means: keep the failing scan as a test.fail expected failure (its failure output lists rule id, impact and targets), record each as a defect, and pin the known violations in a passing test that must be updated when they are fixed.
