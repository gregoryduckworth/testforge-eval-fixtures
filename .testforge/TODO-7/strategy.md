# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The static todo app (public/app.js) lets users tick todos complete, filter by All/Active/Completed and clear completed todos. All state lives in the page (no backend API), so Playwright e2e tests against the real server (node server.js, started by playwright.config.js webServer) prove the user-visible behaviour: strike-through, the #count text, filter results with aria-pressed, and the Clear completed button's visibility and effect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing/uncompleting a todo via its checkbox (strike-through, items-left count)
- All/Active/Completed filters and their pressed state
- Clear completed visibility and effect

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; not in the ticket)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Ticking under the Active filter re-renders and removes the row, so locator.check() can hang re-reading a detached checkbox | medium | Use click() in that case and assert the list contents instead |
| Count text matched page-wide or by substring could pass on wrong values | medium | Assert toHaveText on the #count element exactly |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decreases items left by one

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Page loaded with two todos added

**Steps**
1. Add 'Walk dog' and 'Pay rent'
2. Tick 'Walk dog'

**Expected:** 'Walk dog' title has line-through, 'Pay rent' does not; #count goes from '2 items left' to '1 item left'

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Two todos, one ticked

**Steps**
1. Untick it

**Expected:** Title text-decoration-line is none; #count back to '2 items left'

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Reversal half of AC1

### TC3: Active filter shows only uncompleted todos and is pressed

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC2

**Preconditions**
- Three todos, one completed

**Steps**
1. Click Active

**Expected:** List titles are exactly the two uncompleted todos; Active aria-pressed true, All and Completed false

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent, Call mum

**Rationale:** Active filter

### TC4: Completed filter shows only completed todos and is pressed

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC2

**Preconditions**
- Three todos, one completed

**Steps**
1. Click Completed

**Expected:** List titles are exactly the completed todo; Completed aria-pressed true, others false

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent, Call mum

**Rationale:** Completed filter

### TC5: All filter shows every todo again after another filter and is pressed

*e2e · playwright · positive · medium priority* — covers AC2

**Preconditions**
- Three todos, one completed, Completed filter selected

**Steps**
1. Click All

**Expected:** All three titles listed in order; All aria-pressed true, others false

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent, Call mum

**Rationale:** All filter and switching back

### TC6: completing a todo under the Active filter removes it from the list

*e2e · playwright · edge · medium priority* — covers AC2

**Preconditions**
- Two todos, Active filter selected

**Steps**
1. Click the checkbox of one todo

**Expected:** Only the other todo remains listed; #count '1 item left'

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Filter must react to state changes, not only filter clicks

### TC7: Clear completed is hidden until a todo is completed and hidden again when it is unticked

*e2e · playwright · negative · high priority* — covers AC3

**Preconditions**
- One todo added

**Steps**
1. Check button hidden with the active todo listed
2. Tick todo
3. Check button visible
4. Untick todo

**Expected:** Hidden, then visible, then hidden

**Test data:** Walk dog

**Rationale:** 'appears only while' half of AC3

### TC8: Clear completed removes every completed todo and keeps active ones

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC3

**Preconditions**
- Three todos, two completed

**Steps**
1. Click Clear completed

**Expected:** Only the active todo remains; #count '1 item left'; Clear completed hidden

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent, Call mum

**Rationale:** Removes every completed (more than one) and nothing else

## Environment

playwright.config.js webServer starts node server.js on port 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No data or env vars needed.

## Assumptions

- The app has no backend API: server.js only serves static files and todos live in page memory, so each test starts from an empty list on page load and nothing is faked.
- 'Struck through' is checked as the title span's computed text-decoration-line being line-through (styles.css li.completed span), not via the CSS class.
- The pressed state is the filter button's aria-pressed attribute.
