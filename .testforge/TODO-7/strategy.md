# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The todo app (public/app.js, in-browser state, served by server.js) lets users tick todos complete, filter All/Active/Completed and clear completed ones. The e2e tests drive the real static app in Chromium and prove the strike-through, the items-left count, the filtered lists with aria-pressed state, and the visibility and effect of "Clear completed".

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing and uncompleting a todo via its checkbox
- All/Active/Completed filters and their pressed state
- Clear completed visibility and removal

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps state in memory only)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Ticking a todo's checkbox marks it completed (struck through) and lowers the items-left count by one; unticking reverses both. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | Active shows only uncompleted todos, Completed only completed ones, All every todo; the selected filter is shown as pressed. | TC3, TC4, TC5 |
| AC3 | "Clear completed" is visible only while at least one todo is completed, and clicking it removes every completed todo. | TC6, TC7 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Count or strike-through not updating on untick | medium | TC2 unticks and asserts both revert |
| Filters showing wrong items or pressed state drifting from the active filter | high | TC3-TC5 assert exact title lists and aria-pressed for each filter |
| Clear completed removing active todos or only some completed ones | high | TC7 uses two completed and one active todo and asserts the exact remaining list |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and lowers the items left count by one

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with todos 'Walk the dog' and 'Pay rent' added

**Steps**
1. Check that the count reads '2 items left'
2. Tick 'Walk the dog'

**Expected:** Its checkbox is checked, its title has line-through, 'Pay rent' does not, and the count reads '1 item left'

**Test data:** Walk the dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores the items left count

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with 'Walk the dog' and 'Pay rent', 'Walk the dog' ticked

**Steps**
1. Untick 'Walk the dog'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, no line-through, count reads '2 items left'

**Test data:** Walk the dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Reversal half of AC1

### TC3: Active filter shows only uncompleted todos and is pressed

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC2

**Test data:** three todos

### TC6: Clear completed is hidden until a todo is completed and hidden again when it is unticked

*e2e · playwright · negative · high priority* — covers AC3

### TC7: Clear completed removes every completed todo and keeps the active ones

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC3

**Expected:** List titles are exactly ['Pay rent'], count '1 item left', Clear completed hidden

**Test data:** three todos

**Rationale:** Removal of all (not just one) completed todos

## Environment

Playwright's webServer starts node server.js on 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No data or env vars needed.

## Assumptions

- The real app runs for real via the existing Playwright webServer (node server.js on port 4173); nothing is faked. State lives in page memory, so every test starts from an empty list on a fresh page and needs no cleanup.
- "Struck through" is checked as the computed text-decoration-line: line-through on the todo's title span, which is what the stylesheet applies to completed items.
- The items-left count is read from the #count element (it has no role or test id) with exact text such as '1 item left' / '2 items left'.
