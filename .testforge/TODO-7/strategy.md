# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The todo app (static public/app.js, in-memory state per page load) already supports completing, filtering and clearing todos. The tests must prove in a real browser that ticking/unticking toggles the strike-through and the "items left" count, that the All/Active/Completed filters show the right todos and mark the selected one pressed, and that "Clear completed" shows only while a todo is completed and removes every completed todo.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Checkbox completion and un-completion: strike-through and items-left count
- All / Active / Completed filters and their aria-pressed state
- Visibility and effect of the Clear completed button

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js, TODO earlier ticket)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; the ticket doesn't ask for it)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Ticking a todo's checkbox marks it completed (struck through) and decreases the items-left count by one; unticking reverses both. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | Active shows only uncompleted todos, Completed only completed ones, All every todo; the selected filter is shown as pressed. | TC3, TC4, TC5, TC6 |
| AC3 | "Clear completed" is visible only while at least one todo is completed, and clicking it removes every completed todo. | TC7, TC8 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Count or strike-through not updated when a todo is unticked (only one direction works) | high | TC2 unticks and asserts both the strike-through is gone and the count is back. |
| Filter buttons change the list but not the pressed state, or vice versa | medium | TC3-TC5 assert both list contents and aria-pressed of all three buttons. |
| Clear completed removes only one completed todo or also removes active ones | high | TC7 completes two of three todos and asserts only the active one remains. |
| Locator 'Completed' also matching 'Clear completed' | low | Buttons are located by role with exact names. |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decreases the items left count

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- App open at /

**Steps**
1. Add 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog'
2. Assert '2 items left'
3. Tick 'Buy milk' checkbox

**Expected:** 'Buy milk' checkbox checked and its text has line-through; 'Walk dog' not struck through; count reads '1 item left'.

**Test data:** Two todo titles

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour.

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores the count

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- App open at /

**Steps**
1. Add two todos
2. Tick 'Buy milk', assert '1 item left'
3. Untick 'Buy milk'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, text-decoration-line none, count '2 items left'.

## Environment

No services beyond the static server.js, started by playwright.config.js webServer on port 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No seed data; todos are created through the UI per test.

## Assumptions

- The real app is served by the repo's server.js through the existing playwright.config.js webServer; nothing is faked (there is no backend API).
- State lives in page memory, so each test starts from an empty list after page.goto('/') and creates its own todos through the UI; tests are independent.
- 'Struck through' is checked as the completed todo's text having computed text-decoration-line: line-through (from styles.css), the user-visible effect, rather than the 'completed' class.
- 'Shown as pressed' means aria-pressed="true" on the selected filter button and "false" on the others.
