# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The todo app (public/app.js, client-side in-memory state) lets users tick todos complete, filter All/Active/Completed (aria-pressed on the selected filter button) and clear completed todos. Tests drive the real static server (started by Playwright's existing webServer) in Chromium and prove each acceptance criterion through user-visible state: strike-through, the "N items left" text, the visible list items, aria-pressed, and the Clear completed button's visibility.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing and un-completing a todo (strike-through and items-left count)
- Active/Completed/All filters and their pressed state
- Clear completed visibility and removal

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos only in memory; the ticket does not ask for it)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Count text pluralisation (1 item / 2 items) wrong after toggling | medium | TC1/TC2 assert exact count text both ways across the 1/2 boundary |
| Clear completed removes active todos too, or only one completed todo | high | TC7 completes two of three todos and asserts exactly the active one remains |
| Filter shows wrong set or pressed state not updated | medium | TC3-TC5 assert exact visible list and aria-pressed for all three buttons |

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decrements the items left count

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page at /

**Steps**
1. Add 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog'
2. Assert '2 items left'

## Environment

Playwright's webServer in playwright.config.js starts `node server.js` on port 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). Chromium only. No seed data or env vars.

## Assumptions

- The real app runs: Playwright's existing webServer starts `node server.js` on port 4173; nothing is faked (there is no backend API).
- Todos live in page memory, so each test starts from an empty list on a fresh page and creates its own todos through the UI; no cross-test cleanup is needed.
- "Struck through" is verified as the computed text-decoration-line of the todo's text being line-through (and none once unticked), since that is what the user sees.
- "Pressed" means aria-pressed="true" on the selected filter button and "false" on the others.
