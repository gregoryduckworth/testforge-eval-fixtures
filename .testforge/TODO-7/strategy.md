# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The todo app (static files served by server.js, all state in the browser) lets users tick todos complete, filter All/Active/Completed and clear completed todos. Playwright e2e tests against the real app must prove the strike-through and items-left count follow the checkbox both ways, each filter shows exactly the right todos and is the only one pressed, and "Clear completed" shows only while a todo is completed and removes all completed todos.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing and un-completing a todo via its checkbox (strike-through, items left count)
- All / Active / Completed filters and their aria-pressed state
- Visibility and effect of the Clear completed button

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by the existing add-todo spec, used here only as setup)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; the ticket doesn't ask for it)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Ticking a todo's checkbox marks it completed: it is struck through and the items-left count goes down by one; unticking reverses both. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | Active shows only todos not completed, Completed only completed ones, All shows every todo; the selected filter is shown as pressed. | TC3, TC4, TC5 |
| AC3 | "Clear completed" is shown only while at least one todo is completed, and clicking it removes every completed todo. | TC6, TC7 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Count or strike-through not reverting when a todo is unticked | high | TC2 unticks and checks both revert |
| A filter showing the wrong set, or more than one filter appearing pressed | medium | TC3-TC5 assert the exact list and the aria-pressed state of all three buttons |
| Clear completed removing only the first completed todo, or active todos, or staying visible | high | TC7 completes two of three todos and asserts the exact remaining list and that the button hides |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decrements items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with todos 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog' added

**Steps**
1. Check that '2 items left' shows and 'Buy milk' is not struck through
2. Tick the 'Buy milk' checkbox

**Expected:** 'Buy milk' checkbox is checked and its text is line-through; 'Walk dog' is not; count reads '1 item left'

**Test data:** Buy milk, Walk dog

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores items left

*e2e · playwright · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Todos 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog' added, 'Buy milk' ticked ('1 item left')

**Steps**
1. Untick 'Buy milk'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, text not line-through, count reads '2 items left'

**Test data:** Buy milk, Walk dog

**Rationale:** Reversal is explicitly required

## Environment

Playwright config's webServer starts `node server.js` on port 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No env vars, seed data or backend.

## Assumptions

- The real app runs: Playwright's existing webServer starts `node server.js`; nothing is faked. Todos live in page memory, so each test starts from an empty list and creates its own todos through the UI, with no shared state between tests.
- "Struck through" is checked as the todo text's computed text-decoration-line being line-through (styles.css applies it to li.completed span), and not struck through when active.
- The app has no action this ticket covers that it can refuse; the negative cases are the reverse/absent states (unticking, Clear completed hidden when nothing is completed, filters excluding todos).
