# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

TODO-7 adds completing, filtering and clearing todos to the static todo app (public/app.js; state lives in page memory, so each page load starts empty). Playwright e2e tests against the real app served by server.js must prove that ticking/unticking toggles strike-through and the items-left count, that All/Active/Completed show the right todos and mark the selected filter pressed, and that "Clear completed" shows only while something is completed and removes every completed todo.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Checkbox toggle: strike-through on the title and #count text
- Filter buttons All/Active/Completed: visible todos and aria-pressed
- Clear completed: visibility and removal

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; the ticket doesn't ask for it)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Ticking a todo's checkbox strikes its title through and decreases the items-left count by one; unticking removes the strike-through and increases the count by one. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | Active shows only uncompleted todos, Completed only completed todos, All every todo; the selected filter button has aria-pressed=true and the others false. | TC3, TC4, TC5 |
| AC3 | "Clear completed" is visible only while at least one todo is completed, and clicking it removes every completed todo and no active one. | TC6, TC7 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Count text or strike-through not updated on untick (state drift) | high | TC2 asserts both revert after unticking |
| Clear completed removes only one completed todo or also active ones | high | TC7 completes two of three todos and asserts only the active one remains |
| Filter list re-renders detach the checkbox (check() hangs under Active filter) | low | Tick todos under the All filter before switching filters; assert the list |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decrements the items left count

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with todos 'Walk dog' and 'Pay rent' added

**Steps**
1. Tick 'Walk dog'

**Expected:** 'Walk dog' checkbox checked and its title has line-through; 'Pay rent' title not struck through; #count reads exactly '1 item left' (was '2 items left')

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and increments the items left count

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Todos 'Walk dog' and 'Pay rent', 'Walk dog' ticked ('1 item left')

**Steps**
1. Untick 'Walk dog'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, title text-decoration-line none, #count reads exactly '2 items left'

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Ticket requires reversal of both effects

## Environment

Playwright's webServer in playwright.config.js starts `node server.js` on port 4173 (baseURL http://localhost:4173). No env vars, no seed data; Chromium only.

## Assumptions

- The real app runs for real via the existing playwright.config.js webServer (node server.js on 4173); nothing is faked - there is no backend API.
- Each test opens a fresh page, so todo state is per test; titles are distinct and none is a substring of another.
- "Struck through" is checked as the title span's computed text-decoration-line being line-through (styles.css li.completed span), and absent when unticked.
- None of these actions can be refused by the app (no validation), so there is no refusal case; the negative side is covered by Clear completed being hidden when nothing is completed.
