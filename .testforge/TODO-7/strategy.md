# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The todo app (public/app.js, in-memory per page) lets users tick todos to complete them, filter All/Active/Completed and clear completed todos. Playwright e2e tests against the real static server prove the strike-through and items-left count, each filter's contents and pressed state, and the visibility and effect of "Clear completed".

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing and uncompleting a todo via its checkbox (strike-through, items-left count)
- All / Active / Completed filters and their aria-pressed state
- Clear completed button visibility and effect

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; ticket doesn't ask for it)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |

| AC1 | Ticking a todo's checkbox marks it completed: its title is struck through and the items-left count goes down by one; unticking reverses both. | TC1, TC2 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|

| Clear completed removes active todos or stays visible | medium | TC6, TC7 assert visibility and the remaining todos |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decrements the items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- App open with todos 'Walk dog', 'Pay rent' added

**Steps**
1. Tick 'Walk dog'

**Expected:** Checkbox is checked, 'Walk dog' title has line-through, 'Pay rent' does not; count reads '1 item left' (was '2 items left')

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores the items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Todos 'Walk dog', 'Pay rent' added, 'Walk dog' ticked

**Steps**
1. Untick 'Walk dog'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, title text-decoration not line-through, count reads '2 items left'

**Test data:** Walk dog, Pay rent

**Rationale:** AC1 reversal

### TC3: Active filter shows only todos that are not completed and is pressed

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**

**Expected:** List titles are exactly ['Walk dog', 'Call mum']; Active aria-pressed=true, All and Completed false

**Rationale:** Filter correctness

### TC7: Clear completed is hidden until a todo is completed and hides again when it is unticked

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC3

**Expected:** List titles exactly ['Pay rent', 'Buy bread']; count '2 items left'; Clear completed hidden

**Rationale:** Removes all (not just one) completed todos

## Environment

Playwright's webServer starts `node server.js` on 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No env vars or seed data.

## Assumptions

- The app's only server is server.js serving static files; it runs for real via Playwright's existing webServer config. Nothing is faked.
- Todos live in page memory, so each test gets fresh state from its own page; no cleanup is needed.
- 'Struck through' is checked as the computed text-decoration-line of the todo's title (line-through), which the app's stylesheet applies to li.completed span.
- 'Shown as pressed' means aria-pressed="true" on the selected filter button and "false" on the others, as app.js sets it.
