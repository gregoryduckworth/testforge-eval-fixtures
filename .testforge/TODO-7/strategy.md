# Test strategy: TODO-7 Complete, filter and clear todos

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

The static todo app (public/app.js) already lets users tick todos complete, filter by All/Active/Completed and clear completed todos, all in in-memory page state. The tests prove, in the browser, that ticking strikes the todo through and decrements "items left" (and unticking reverses both), that each filter shows the right todos and is pressed, and that "Clear completed" is shown only while a todo is completed and removes every completed one.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Completing/uncompleting a todo via its checkbox: strike-through and items-left count
- All / Active / Completed filters and their aria-pressed state
- Clear completed visibility and removal of every completed todo

**Out of scope**
- Adding todos (covered by tests/add-todo.spec.js, used only as setup)
- Persistence across reloads (the app keeps todos in memory only; not asked by the ticket)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |

| AC3 | "Clear completed" is visible only while at least one todo is completed, and clicking it removes every completed todo and keeps the active ones. | TC6, TC7 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Items-left count wrong after toggling (pluralisation or off-by-one) | high | TC1/TC2 assert the exact count text before and after ticking and unticking |
| Filter shows wrong todos | medium | TC3–TC5 assert the exact list per filter with a mix of active and completed todos |
| Clear completed removes active todos or leaves some completed ones | high | TC7 uses two completed and one active todo and asserts exactly the active one remains |

## Test case detail

### TC1: ticking a todo strikes it through and decrements items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with todos 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog' added

**Steps**
1. Expect '2 items left' and 'Buy milk' not struck through
2. Tick the checkbox labelled 'Buy milk'

**Expected:** Checkbox is checked, 'Buy milk' text has line-through, 'Walk dog' does not, count reads '1 item left'

**Test data:** Buy milk, Walk dog

**Rationale:** Core completion behaviour

### TC2: unticking a completed todo removes the strike-through and restores items left

*e2e · playwright · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with 'Buy milk' and 'Walk dog', 'Buy milk' ticked ('1 item left')

**Steps**
1. Untick 'Buy milk'

**Expected:** Checkbox unchecked, text-decoration-line none, count '2 items left'

**Test data:** Buy milk, Walk dog

**Rationale:** Reverse transition of AC1

### TC3: Active filter shows only todos that are not completed and is pressed

### TC6: Clear completed is hidden until a todo is completed and hidden again when none is

*e2e · playwright · negative · high priority* — covers AC3

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with 'Buy milk' added

**Steps**
1. Expect '1 item left' (list loaded) and Clear completed hidden
2. Tick 'Buy milk', expect it visible
3. Untick 'Buy milk'

**Expected:** Clear completed button hidden, then visible, then hidden again

**Test data:** Buy milk

**Rationale:** Refusal side: the action isn't offered when nothing is completed

### TC7: Clear completed removes every completed todo and keeps active ones

*e2e · playwright · positive · critical priority* — covers AC3

**Preconditions**
- Fresh page with 'Buy milk', 'Walk dog', 'Pay rent'; 'Buy milk' and 'Pay rent' ticked

**Steps**
1. Click Clear completed

**Expected:** List is exactly ['Walk dog'], count '1 item left', Clear completed hidden

**Test data:** three todos, two completed

**Rationale:** Removal must hit every completed todo, not just one

## Environment

Playwright's webServer in playwright.config.js starts `node server.js` on port 4173 (BASE_URL http://localhost:4173). No data or services beyond that.

## Assumptions

- No backend: server.js only serves static files and todos live in page memory, so each test gets a fresh, empty list from its own page; nothing is faked and the real server started by playwright.config.js webServer is used.
- "Struck through" is checked as the todo text's computed text-decoration-line being line-through (styles.css: li.completed span), since there is no accessible equivalent.
- "Pressed" means aria-pressed="true" on the selected filter button and "false" on the others (app.js render).
