# Test strategy: SHOP-40 Tests for pull request #130: coupon codes, and a discount for buying in bulk

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

PR #130 adds coupon codes (SAVE10 = 10% off rounded down, TENOFF = £10 off capped at the amount left) applied by priceBasket after the spend/bulk discount, case-insensitive matching, UnknownCouponError for unknown codes, and a bulk discount (10+ items → 10%, taking the larger of bulk and spend discounts). It also refactors formatPence without changing output. Unit tests with Vitest prove each rule through couponDiscount, discountFor and priceBasket, plus formatPence edge outputs.

## Scope

**In scope**
- couponDiscount in src/coupons.js
- discountFor and priceBasket in src/pricing.js (bulk discount, coupon after discount, coupon field in the result)
- formatPence output after the tidy-up

**Out of scope**
- Any checkout UI (none exists in this repo)

## Assumptions

- Amounts are whole pence and percentage discounts round down, as the code's Math.floor and doc comments say.
- Surrounding spaces in a code are trimmed (code comment says so; ticket silent) — tested as the code's behaviour.
- An empty/absent coupon means no coupon (priceBasket only applies a truthy coupon); not asserted as the ticket says nothing about it.
