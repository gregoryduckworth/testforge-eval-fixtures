## Summary

Commit 72802a9 (Show each line's product name on an order) made GET /orders/:id slower: the k6 `detail` scenario in perf/orders.js went from p95 22 ms to 30 ms and p99 23 ms to 31 ms, and a rerun confirmed it. The diff adds a third store query, `productNames`, after `orderLines`. It runs in sequence, so each detail request makes one more round trip of 6–9 ms. The guard adds `detail`-scoped p95 and p99 thresholds to perf/orders.js at the baseline plus 20%. It records the defect and files an issue for a person to fix.
