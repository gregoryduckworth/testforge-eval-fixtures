# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

cartTotal in src/cart.js prices a cart with the bulk discount (10% at 10+ items summed over lines), the SAVE5 coupon (after the bulk discount, goods floored at $0, other codes refused) and shipping ($4.99 unless discounted goods reach $50; empty cart costs 0). Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule at its boundaries. Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount — that case is expected to fail and will be recorded as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal and bulkDiscount in src/cart.js: bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, unknown coupon refusal, shipping threshold, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- UI/checkout pages (the repo is a pure pricing module)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 100 subtotal less 10 = 90, plus 4.99 shipping = 94.99 (currently 100: expected to fail, defect)

**Test data:** lines {10 x4}, {10 x6}

**Rationale:** Boundary of AC1; code uses > 10.

### TC2: gives 10% off a cart of 11 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:5},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 110 less 11 = 99, free shipping: 99

**Rationale:** Passing guard that quantities are summed across lines.

### TC3: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:5}])

**Expected:** 90 (no discount, free shipping)

**Rationale:** Just below the threshold.

### TC4: takes $5 off with SAVE5 after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:11}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** 110 - 11 - 5 = 94 (coupon first would give 94.5)

**Rationale:** Proves order of discounts.

### TC5: never prices the goods below $0 with SAVE5

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:1}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** goods 0 plus 4.99 shipping = 4.99 (not 2.99)

**Rationale:** Floor at zero.

### TC6: refuses the coupon code %j with an error

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. call cartTotal once with a non-SAVE5 code (it.each: 'SAVE10', 'save5', ''), capture the thrown error

**Expected:** throws an Error whose message names the submitted code

**Test data:** 'SAVE10', 'save5', ''

**Rationale:** Refusal path of the coupon.

### TC7: ships free when the goods cost exactly $50

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC3

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes are matched exactly (case-sensitive), as the code does: 'save5' is another code and is refused.
- 'Goods' means subtotal less bulk discount less coupon, floored at 0; amounts are rounded to cents by the module.
- Tests go in a new file test/checkout.test.js beside test/cart.test.js and run under the existing `npm test` (vitest run).
