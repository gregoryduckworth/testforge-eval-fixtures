# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout via cartTotal(items, { coupon }) in src/cart.js: 10% bulk discount at 10+ items (quantities summed), SAVE5 coupon ($5 after bulk, floored at $0, other codes refused), $4.99 shipping unless discounted goods are >= $50, empty cart free. Unit tests with Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount — a suspected defect against AC1, kept as an expected failure.

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API for checkout (none exists in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 100 subtotal - 10 = 90 (free shipping). Currently returns 100: expected failure (it.fails) with record_defect.

**Test data:** lines of quantity 4 and 6 at $10

**Rationale:** Threshold boundary named by the AC; exposes the > vs >= defect

### TC2: gives 10% off a cart of 11 items spread over several lines

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: only exactly 'SAVE5' is valid (code uses an exact key lookup); 'save5' is refused.
- 'Refused with an error' means cartTotal throws; tests check the thrown message includes the submitted code (the part the test controls).
- Amounts are compared as rounded-to-cents numbers as cartTotal returns them.
- Pure unit tests; nothing is faked.
