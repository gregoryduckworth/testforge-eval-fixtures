# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout with a 10% bulk discount (10+ items), the SAVE5 coupon (applied after the bulk discount, floored at $0, unknown codes refused) and shipping ($4.99, free from $50 of discounted goods, nothing for an empty cart). src/cart.js already implements this in cartTotal/bulkDiscount; unit tests through cartTotal must prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code shows bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so exactly 10 items get no discount, contradicting AC1; that case is expected to fail and will be reported as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal(items, { coupon }) in src/cart.js: bulk discount, coupon, shipping, empty cart
- Boundaries: 9/10 items, $50 of discounted goods, coupon larger than the goods

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API around checkout (none exists in the repo)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | A cart with 10 or more items in total (sum of line quantities) gets 10% off its subtotal; fewer items get no bulk discount. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | Coupon SAVE5 takes $5 off after the bulk discount, the goods never cost less than $0, and any other coupon code is refused with an error. | TC3, TC4, TC5 |
| AC3 | Shipping is free when goods cost $50 or more after discounts, otherwise $4.99; an empty cart costs $0. | TC6, TC7, TC8 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one on the bulk threshold (already present in the code) | high | TC1 (exactly 10 items over several lines) and TC2 (9 items) |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount changes the price | medium | TC3 uses amounts where the order gives different totals |
| Shipping decided on the subtotal instead of discounted goods | medium | TC6 reaches exactly $50 only after the coupon |

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Expected to fail: code uses > 10.

**Test data:** two lines, quantities 4 and 6 at $10

**Rationale:** Threshold boundary and the quantity-sum rule

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Rationale:** Below-threshold side of the boundary

### TC3: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**

## Environment

Pure unit tests; no services. Run with `npm test` (vitest run).

## Assumptions

- Totals are rounded to cents as cartTotal does; amounts are compared with toBe on values chosen to be exact in cents
- Coupon codes are case-sensitive (only the exact code SAVE5 is accepted), as the code does
- When goods are floored at $0 for a non-empty cart, shipping of $4.99 still applies (goods < $50), as the code does

## Open questions

- AC1 says 10 or more items get the discount; the code applies it only above 10 (`>` instead of `>=`). Tests follow the ticket and mark the 10-item case expected-to-fail.
