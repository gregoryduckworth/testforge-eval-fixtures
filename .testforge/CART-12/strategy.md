# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

src/cart.js already implements cartTotal (bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, shipping). Unit tests with Vitest must prove each AC through the public functions cartTotal/bulkDiscount, including the boundaries (10 items, $50, $0 floor) and refusal of unknown coupons. Reading the code shows bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, against AC1 and the constant's own doc comment ("at least this many"): that case is expected to fail and will be recorded as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal and bulkDiscount in src/cart.js: bulk discount threshold, coupon handling, $0 floor, shipping threshold, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Line-total/subtotal behaviour already covered by test/cart.test.js
- Any UI or checkout endpoint (none exists in the repo)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one at the bulk threshold (exactly 10 items) | high | TC1 tests exactly 10 items; TC2 tests 9. |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount, or shipping threshold computed before discounts | high | TC4 picks amounts where each wrong order gives a different total; TC7 reaches $50 only after the coupon. |
| Negative goods total when the coupon exceeds the goods | medium | TC5. |

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{ price: 3, quantity: 10 }])

**Expected:** 31.99 (30 - 3 discount + 4.99 shipping). Expected to fail today: code returns 34.99 (defect, marked it.fails).

**Test data:** 10 x $3

**Rationale:** Boundary of AC1; the code uses > instead of >=.

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{ price: 3, quantity: 9 }])

**Expected:** 31.99 (27 + 4.99)

**Test data:** 9 x $3

**Rationale:** Just below the threshold.

### TC3: counts items across lines by quantity for the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{ price: 2, quantity: 6 }, { price: 2, quantity: 5 }])

**Expected:** 24.79 (22 - 2.20 + 4.99)

**Test data:** two lines, 11 items

**Rationale:** Proves quantities are summed, not lines counted; 11 avoids the boundary defect.

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2, AC3

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{ price: 5, quantity: 12 }], { coupon: 'SAVE5' })

**Expected:** 53.99 (60 - 6 - 5 = 49 goods, below $50 so + 4.99). Coupon-first would give 54.49.

**Test data:** 12 x $5, SAVE5

**Rationale:** Order of discounts and shipping judged after discounts.

## Environment

Pure unit tests, no services. Run with `npm test` (vitest run).

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: 'save5' counts as 'any other coupon code' and is refused (the code does this).
- Refusal with an error means cartTotal throws; the test checks the error names the submitted code rather than matching the whole wording.
- Amounts are compared rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns them.
