# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout via cartTotal/bulkDiscount in src/cart.js: 10% bulk discount at 10+ items (summed quantities), SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, goods floored at $0, other codes refused), and $4.99 shipping below $50 of discounted goods (free at/above, empty cart costs 0). Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries through the public functions.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal and bulkDiscount in src/cart.js
- Bulk discount threshold and quantity summing
- SAVE5 ordering, $0 floor, refusal of other codes
- Shipping threshold after discounts and empty cart

**Out of scope**
- UI/checkout pages (none exist in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart with exactly 10 items

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:6,quantity:10}])

**Expected:** 54 (60 less 6; free shipping)

**Test data:** 1 line, $6 x 10

**Rationale:** Threshold boundary; code reads > so likely defect

### TC2: counts items by adding up each line's quantity

## Environment

Pure unit tests; no services or env vars. Run with `npm test`.

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes match exactly (case-sensitive): 'save5' is another code and is refused.
- Checkout total = discounted goods + shipping, rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns.
- Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so exactly 10 items gets no discount; tests asserting AC1 at the boundary are expected to fail and will be reported as a defect.
- `coupon in COUPONS` accepts inherited names like 'toString'; the refusal test for it is expected to fail and will be reported.
