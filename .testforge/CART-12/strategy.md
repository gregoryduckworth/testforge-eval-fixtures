# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart in src/cart.js `cartTotal(items, { coupon })`: 10% bulk discount at 10+ total items, SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, goods floored at $0, other codes throw), and $4.99 shipping below $50 of discounted goods (free at/above, empty cart $0). Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code, the bulk check uses `>` instead of `>=` (10 items get no discount) and the coupon lookup uses `in`, so prototype keys like `toString` are accepted; those cases are expected to fail and reported as defects.

## Assumptions

- Item count is the sum of line quantities; prices are dollars as numbers; amounts are rounded to cents by the module.
- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: only exactly 'SAVE5' is valid.
- Refusing a coupon means cartTotal throws; the test checks the thrown message contains the submitted code (the part the test controls).
- Shipping applies to a non-empty cart even when the goods drop to $0 with SAVE5.
