# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout through `cartTotal(items, { coupon })` in src/cart.js: a 10% bulk discount at 10+ items (summing quantities), the SAVE5 coupon applied after the bulk discount with goods floored at $0, refusal of any other coupon, and $4.99 shipping below $50 of discounted goods (free at $50+, nothing for an empty cart). Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundary through cartTotal. Reading the code, `bulkDiscount` uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, against AC1; that case is kept as an expected failure and reported as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal pricing: bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, unknown coupons, shipping threshold, empty cart
- Boundaries: 9/10/11 items, goods exactly $50 after discounts, coupon larger than goods

**Out of scope**
- UI or API checkout flows (the repo is a pure pricing library)

## Assumptions

- Totals are compared as numbers rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns them (cents()).
- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: 'save5' is 'any other coupon code' and is refused, as the code does.
- A non-empty cart whose goods drop to $0 through the coupon still pays $4.99 shipping (goods < $50; only an empty cart costs nothing).
