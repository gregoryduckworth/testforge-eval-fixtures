# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout with three promotions in src/cart.js `cartTotal`: 10% bulk discount at 10+ items (summing quantities), the SAVE5 coupon applied after the bulk discount with goods floored at $0 (other codes throw), and $4.99 shipping unless discounted goods reach $50 (empty cart is free). Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries through `cartTotal`. Reading the code, `bulkDiscount` uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, contrary to AC1 and to the constant's own doc comment ("at least this many"); that case is expected to fail and will be recorded as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal pricing: bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, unknown coupons, shipping threshold, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Coupon case-sensitivity and stacking multiple coupons (not in the ticket)
- UI/checkout flow beyond the pricing function (repo has only the pricing module)

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|

| Shipping threshold checked on the pre-discount subtotal | medium | TC7 and TC9 use carts where discounts cross the $50 line |

## Environment

Pure unit tests; no services. Run with `npm test`.

## Assumptions

- Prices are in dollars as plain numbers; totals are rounded to cents by cartTotal
- A non-empty cart whose goods are $0 after the coupon still pays $4.99 shipping, since AC3 only waives shipping at $50+ or for an empty cart
- Tests import the thresholds' behaviour through cartTotal and assert concrete dollar amounts computed from the ticket

## Open questions
