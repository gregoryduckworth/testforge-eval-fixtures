# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout: 10% bulk discount at 10+ items (summed quantities), the SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, floored at $0, other codes refused), and $4.99 shipping unless discounted goods reach $50 (empty cart costs $0). The logic lives in src/cart.js (bulkDiscount, cartTotal); unit tests with Vitest must prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart with exactly 10 items looks like it gets no discount; the boundary test should expose this.

## Scope

**In scope**
- bulkDiscount and cartTotal in src/cart.js
- Bulk threshold boundaries (9/10/11 items across several lines)
- SAVE5 ordering after bulk discount, $0 floor, refusal of other codes
- Free shipping threshold on discounted goods, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- UI / checkout page (none in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart with exactly 10 items across lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:4},{price:2,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 24 - 2.40 + 4.99 = 26.59

**Test data:** 10 items, subtotal $24

**Rationale:** Lower boundary of the bulk rule, summing quantities over lines

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart with 9 items

## Environment

No services; pure unit tests run by `npm test`.

## Assumptions

- Amounts are rounded to cents by the app; tests compare exact cent values with toBe.
- A refused coupon is signalled by cartTotal throwing an Error whose message names the submitted code.
- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: 'save5' is 'any other coupon code' and is refused.
- The coupon is applied to an empty cart without error and the total stays $0.

## Open questions

- Should coupon codes be case-insensitive (is 'save5' valid)? Tests assume they are case-sensitive, as the code is.
