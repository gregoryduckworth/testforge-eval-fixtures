# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout: a 10% bulk discount at 10+ total items, the SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, goods floored at $0, other codes refused), and $4.99 shipping unless discounted goods reach $50 (empty cart costs nothing). The logic lives in src/cart.js (cartTotal, bulkDiscount); unit tests through cartTotal must prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount — likely a defect the boundary case will expose.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal / bulkDiscount pricing in src/cart.js
- Bulk discount threshold boundary (9, 10, 11 items) counted by quantity across lines
- SAVE5 ordering after bulk discount, $0 floor, unknown-code refusal
- Free shipping boundary at $50 after discounts, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API for checkout (none exists in the repo)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one at the bulk threshold (code uses > 10 instead of >= 10) | high | TC1 tests exactly 10 items; TC2/TC3 bracket it |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount, changing totals | medium | TC4 uses amounts where the order changes the result |
| Shipping decided on subtotal rather than discounted goods | medium | TC10 uses a subtotal ≥ $50 that falls below after the bulk discount |

## Environment

Pure unit tests; run with `npm test` (vitest run). No services or env vars.

## Assumptions

- cartTotal(items, { coupon }) is the checkout price; refusal of a coupon is a thrown Error
- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: 'save5' is 'another code' and is refused
- When SAVE5 floors goods at $0 on a non-empty cart, shipping of $4.99 still applies (goods < $50)
- Expected totals are computed by hand in the tests from the ticket's rules

## Open questions

- Should a non-empty cart whose goods are floored to $0 by SAVE5 still pay $4.99 shipping? Tests assume yes, as the code does.
- Should coupon codes be case-insensitive? Tests assume not.
