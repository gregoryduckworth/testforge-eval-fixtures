# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart with a bulk discount (10% off at 10+ items summed over line quantities), the SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, floored at $0, any other code refused) and shipping ($4.99, free when discounted goods reach $50, nothing for an empty cart). All of it lives in pure functions in src/cart.js (cartTotal, bulkDiscount), so Vitest unit tests prove every rule at its boundaries. Reading the code shows bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, contrary to AC1 and the function's own doc comment; that case will be kept as an expected failure and reported as a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal and bulkDiscount in src/cart.js: bulk discount threshold, coupon order/floor/refusal, shipping threshold, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API checkout flow (the repo has only the pricing module)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one at the bulk threshold: code uses > 10 instead of >= 10, so exactly 10 items get no discount. | high | TC1 asserts the discount at exactly 10 items (expected failure + defect); TC2/TC3 pin 9 and 11. |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount gives a different total. | medium | TC4 uses amounts where the two orders differ (94 vs 94.5). |
| Shipping threshold evaluated on subtotal rather than discounted goods. | medium | TC9 and TC10 use carts whose subtotal is >= $50 but discounted goods are < $50. |

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:6},{price:10,quantity:4}])

**Expected:** Total is 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Currently returns 100: expected failure, defect recorded.

**Test data:** two lines of $10, quantities 6 and 4

**Rationale:** Lower boundary of the bulk rule and summing quantities across lines; code has an off-by-one.

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:5},{price:10,quantity:4}])

**Expected:** Total is 90 (no discount, free shipping)

**Rationale:** Just below the threshold.

### TC3: gives 10% off a cart of 11 items

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:11}])

**Expected:** Total is 99

**Rationale:** Above the threshold.

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:11}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** Total is 94 (110 -> 99 -> 94, free shipping), not 94.5

**Rationale:** Proves order of discounts.

### TC5: never lets SAVE5 take the goods below $0

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:1}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** Total is 4.99 (goods $0 plus shipping)

**Rationale:** Floor at zero.

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: only exactly 'SAVE5' is accepted (code uses an exact key lookup; the ticket says any other code is refused).
- Totals are compared as numbers rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns them.
- Tests import thresholds/amounts only implicitly via expected totals computed from the ticket's numbers, so a changed constant that breaks the ticket's rule fails the test.
