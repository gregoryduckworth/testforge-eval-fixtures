# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 2 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout with a bulk discount (10% off at 10+ items), the SAVE5 coupon (applied after the bulk discount, goods floored at $0, other codes refused) and shipping ($4.99 below $50 of discounted goods, free from $50, nothing for an empty cart). Unit tests on cartTotal in src/cart.js prove each rule and its boundaries. bulkDiscount uses `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, contrary to AC1; TC1 is expected to fail and is reported as a defect. Revision 2 adds TC11 (review finding R1.1): shipping is charged when the subtotal is $50+ but the discounted goods are under $50.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal in src/cart.js: bulk discount threshold, SAVE5 coupon order and floor, unknown coupon refusal, shipping threshold on discounted goods, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API for checkout (none exists in the repository)

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |

| Shipping threshold computed on the pre-discount subtotal or with > instead of >=. | medium | TC11 has a $50+ subtotal with goods under $50 after the coupon; TC8/TC9 test $50.00 and $49.99. |

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread across lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. Price a cart of $10 x 4 and $10 x 6 with cartTotal

**Expected:** Total is 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Expected to fail against current code (> instead of >=): marked expected-to-fail and reported as a defect.

**Test data:** [{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}]

**Rationale:** Boundary of the bulk threshold, counting quantities across lines.

## Assumptions

- Coupon codes are case-sensitive: the code only knows 'SAVE5', so 'save5' is 'any other coupon code' and is refused.
- A non-empty cart whose goods fall to $0 after the coupon still pays $4.99 shipping (AC3 only exempts an empty cart and goods of $50 or more), as the code does.
- Money is compared in dollars as numbers rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns them.
