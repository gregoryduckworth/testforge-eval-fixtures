# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout with a bulk discount (10% off at 10+ items summed across lines), the SAVE5 coupon ($5 off after the bulk discount, floored at $0, other codes refused) and shipping ($4.99, free when discounted goods reach $50, nothing for an empty cart). The tests exercise the exported `cartTotal` in src/cart.js at unit level with Vitest. Reading the code, `bulkDiscount` uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD` instead of `>=`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount; TC1 will expose this and be kept as an expected failure with a recorded defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal pricing: bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, unknown coupons, shipping thresholds, empty cart
- Boundaries: 9/10 items, $49.99/$50 goods, coupon larger than goods

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or checkout flow beyond the pricing module (the repo contains only src/cart.js)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:2,quantity:6},{price:2,quantity:4}])

**Expected:** 20 - 2 = 18 goods + 4.99 shipping = 22.99 (expected to fail: code requires more than 10)

**Test data:** two lines, quantities 6 and 4 at $2

**Rationale:** Boundary of the bulk rule and item count summed across lines.

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:2,quantity:9}])

**Expected:** 18 + 4.99 = 22.99

**Test data:** 9 × $2

**Rationale:** Lower side of the threshold.

### TC3: gives 10% off a cart of more than 10 items

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:5,quantity:12}])

**Expected:** 60 - 6 = 54, free shipping, total 54

**Test data:** 12 × $5

**Rationale:** Happy path of the bulk discount.

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:5,quantity:12}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** 60 - 6 - 5 = 49 + 4.99 = 53.99 (coupon first would give 54.49)

**Test data:** 12 × $5, SAVE5

**Rationale:** Proves the order of discounts.

### TC5: never lets SAVE5 take the goods below $0

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:1}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** goods 0 + shipping 4.99 = 4.99

**Test data:** 1 × $3, SAVE5

**Rationale:** Floor at zero.

### TC6: refuses an unknown coupon code with an error

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:5,quantity:1}], {coupon:'SAVE10'})

**Expected:** throws an Error whose message names SAVE10

**Test data:** SAVE10

**Rationale:** Refusal path of the coupon.

### TC7: ships free when the goods cost exactly $50

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC3

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:25,quantity:2}])

**Expected:** 50

**Test data:** 2 × $25

**Rationale:** Upper boundary of shipping.

### TC8: charges $4.99 shipping when the goods cost $49.99

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC3

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:49.99,quantity:1}])

**Expected:** 54.98

**Test data:** 1 × $49.99

**Rationale:** Lower boundary of shipping.

### TC9: decides free shipping on the goods after discounts

*unit · vitest · edge · medium priority* — covers AC3

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:4.5,quantity:12}])

**Expected:** 54 subtotal - 5.4 = 48.6 + 4.99 = 53.59

**Test data:** 12 × $4.50

**Rationale:** Shipping must use the discounted amount.

### TC10: charges nothing for an empty cart, even with SAVE5

*unit · vitest · edge · medium priority* — covers AC3

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([])
2. cartTotal([], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** both 0

**Test data:** empty cart

**Rationale:** Empty cart must not be charged shipping.

## Environment

No services; pure unit tests run with `npm test` (vitest run).

## Assumptions

- The checkout price is what `cartTotal(items, { coupon })` returns, rounded to cents; tests assert its return value.
- A refused coupon is signalled by `cartTotal` throwing an Error; tests check it names the submitted code (the part the test controls).
- Constants (BULK_THRESHOLD etc.) are imported from src/cart.js where the test needs them, rather than repeated.
