# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 2 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

cartTotal(items, {coupon}) in src/cart.js prices a cart: bulk 10% off at the item-count threshold, SAVE5 coupon after it (floored at $0), unknown coupons throw (exact, case-sensitive match), $4.99 shipping below $50 of discounted goods, empty cart free. Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries. bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so exactly 10 items get no discount, against AC1; that case is an expected failure with a recorded defect. Revision 2 adds two cases from PR review: the coupon taking goods below $50 brings shipping back, and lower-case save5 is refused.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal, bulkDiscount behaviour in src/cart.js via its exported API

**Out of scope**
- UI/checkout pages (none exist in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread across lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Currently returns 100: expected failure + defect.

**Test data:** 4 + 6 items at $10

**Rationale:** Boundary of AC1; code uses > threshold.
