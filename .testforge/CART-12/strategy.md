# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout in src/cart.js: bulk discount (10% off at 10+ items summed over quantities), the SAVE5 coupon applied after it with a $0 floor on goods, unknown coupons refused, and shipping ($4.99, free from $50 after discounts, nothing for an empty cart). Unit tests against bulkDiscount/cartTotal must prove each rule and its boundaries. Reading the code shows `itemCount > BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount — a suspected defect against AC1.

## Scope

**In scope**
- bulkDiscount and cartTotal in src/cart.js
- Boundaries: 9/10 items, $50 goods, $0 floor
- Coupon refusal

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API for checkout (none exists in the repo)

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | A cart with 10 or more items in total (sum of line quantities) gets 10% off its subtotal; fewer gets none. | TC1, TC2 |
| AC2 | SAVE5 takes $5 off after the bulk discount, goods never below $0; any other coupon code is refused with an error. | TC3, TC4, TC5 |
| AC3 | Shipping is free when goods after discounts are $50 or more, otherwise $4.99; an empty cart costs $0. | TC6, TC7, TC8, TC9 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one at the 10-item threshold (code uses > instead of >=) | high | TC1 asserts exactly 10 items spread over lines get 10% off; kept as expected-fail with a defect if it fails |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount gives a different total | medium | TC3 uses amounts where the order changes the total |
| Shipping threshold checked on the subtotal instead of discounted goods | medium | TC6 and TC9 put the subtotal above $50 and discounted goods at/below it |
