# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout in `cartTotal` (src/cart.js): a 10% bulk discount at 10+ items, then the SAVE5 coupon (floored at $0, other codes refused), then shipping ($4.99 unless discounted goods reach $50, nothing for an empty cart). Unit tests on `cartTotal` must prove each rule and its boundaries, including the order of discounts. Reading the code, the bulk rule uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD` while the ticket and the constant's own doc comment say "at least 10", so a cart of exactly 10 items is expected to fail (suspected defect).

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal, with bulkDiscount/itemCount/subtotal exercised through it
- Bulk threshold boundary (9/10 items), item count summed across lines
- Coupon order, floor at $0, refusal of unknown codes
- Shipping threshold boundary after discounts, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API; the repo is a pure pricing library

## Acceptance criteria coverage

| AC | Criterion | Test cases |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | A cart with 10 or more items in total (sum of line quantities) gets 10% off its subtotal; fewer than 10 gets none. | TC1, TC2, TC3 |
| AC2 | SAVE5 takes $5 off after the bulk discount, goods never cost less than $0, and any other coupon code is refused with an error. | TC4, TC5, TC6 |
| AC3 | Shipping is free when goods cost $50 or more after discounts, otherwise $4.99; an empty cart costs $0. | TC5, TC7, TC8, TC9, TC10 |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Off-by-one on the bulk threshold (code uses > 10 instead of >= 10) | high | TC1 tests exactly 10 items; kept as expected failure with a recorded defect |
| Coupon applied before the bulk discount, changing totals | medium | TC4 uses amounts where the two orders give different totals |
| Shipping judged on the pre-discount subtotal | medium | TC7 and TC9 put the threshold on the discounted amount |

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Expected to fail: code requires more than 10 items.

**Test data:** two lines, 10 items, $100

**Rationale:** Boundary of the bulk rule; the code appears off by one

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:9}])

**Expected:** 90

**Test data:** 9 × $10

**Rationale:** Just below the threshold

### TC3: counts items by adding up each line's quantity

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Expected:** 54 (60 less 10%)

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**

**Expected:** 103 (120 − 12 − 5); coupon first would give 103.5

**Rationale:** Proves discount order

### TC5: never prices goods below $0 with SAVE5

## Assumptions

- The prices in the tests are chosen so float rounding can't affect the threshold comparisons
- An unknown coupon is refused by cartTotal throwing an Error; tests check the error names the submitted code rather than the full wording
