# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout via cartTotal(items, { coupon }) in src/cart.js: 10% bulk discount at 10+ items, SAVE5 coupon applied after the bulk discount and floored at $0, unknown coupons refused, free shipping from $50 of discounted goods otherwise $4.99, empty cart costs $0. Unit tests in Vitest prove each rule and its boundaries through the public cartTotal function. Reading the code, the bulk discount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD` (strictly more than 10) while the ticket and the constant's own doc comment say "at least" 10, so the 10-item boundary case is expected to expose a defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal pricing: bulk discount, SAVE5 coupon, unknown coupon refusal, shipping threshold, empty cart
- Boundaries: 9 vs 10 items, $50.00 vs $49.99 goods, coupon larger than goods

**Out of scope**
- Any UI or API for checkout (none exists in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:4},{price:3,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 31.99 (30 - 3 bulk + 4.99 shipping)

**Test data:** 2 lines, 10 items, $30 subtotal

**Rationale:** Boundary of the bulk rule and the sum of quantities across lines; the code uses > 10, so this likely exposes a defect.

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:4},{price:3,quantity:5}])

**Expected:** 31.99 (27 + 4.99 shipping)

**Test data:** 9 items at $3

**Rationale:** Below-threshold side of the boundary.

### TC3: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:5,quantity:12}], { coupon: 'SAVE5' })

**Expected:** 53.99 (60 - 6 = 54, - 5 = 49 goods, + 4.99 shipping); coupon-first would give 54.49

**Test data:** 12 items at $5

**Rationale:** Proves order of discounts.

### TC4: never prices the goods below $0 when SAVE5 exceeds them

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:1}], { coupon: 'SAVE5' })

**Expected:** 4.99 (goods 0 + shipping)

**Test data:** one $3 item

**Rationale:** Floor at $0.

### TC5: refuses an unknown coupon code with an error

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC3

**Steps**

**Expected:** Throws an Error whose message names SAVE10

**Test data:** coupon SAVE10

**Rationale:** Refusal case for the coupon action.

### TC6: ships free when the goods cost exactly $50 after discounts

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC4

**Rationale:** Threshold inclusive and measured after discounts.

### TC7: charges $4.99 shipping when the goods cost $49.99

## Assumptions

- cartTotal(items, { coupon }) in src/cart.js is the checkout pricing entry point; items are { price, quantity }.
- Totals are compared as numbers rounded to cents, as cartTotal returns them.
- Coupon codes are matched exactly (case-sensitive); only 'SAVE5' is tested as valid.
