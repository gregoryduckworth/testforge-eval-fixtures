# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

CART-12 prices the cart at checkout in src/cart.js: cartTotal applies a 10% bulk discount (10+ items by summed quantity), then the SAVE5 coupon ($5 off, goods floored at $0, other codes throw), then shipping ($4.99 unless discounted goods ≥ $50; empty cart free). Tests are vitest unit tests on cartTotal/bulkDiscount, focused on the boundaries. Reading the code, bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so a cart of exactly 10 items gets no discount, contrary to AC1 and to the constant's own doc comment ("at least this many"); that case is planned as an expected failure with a recorded defect.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal and bulkDiscount in src/cart.js: bulk discount threshold and rate, coupon order and floor, unknown coupon refusal, shipping threshold on discounted goods, empty cart

**Out of scope**
- UI/checkout pages (none exist in the repo)
- Coupon case-sensitivity and whitespace handling (not specified by the ticket)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread across lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** Total 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Currently the code returns 100 (uses > 10), so the test is marked expected-to-fail and a defect recorded.

**Test data:** two lines, quantities 4 and 6, $10 each

**Rationale:** Boundary of the bulk rule; also proves quantities are summed across lines.

### TC2: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:9}])

**Expected:** Total 90 (no discount, free shipping at exactly 90 ≥ 50).

**Test data:** one line, 9 × $10

**Rationale:** Just below the threshold.

### TC3: gives 10% off a cart of 11 items

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:5},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** Total 99 (110 less 11).

**Test data:** quantities 5 and 6 at $10

**Rationale:** Happy path above the threshold, independent of the boundary defect.

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:11}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** Total 94 (110 − 11 − 5); coupon-first would give 94.5.

**Test data:** 11 × $10, coupon SAVE5

**Rationale:** Proves the ordering of discounts.

### TC5: never prices the goods below $0 with SAVE5

*unit · vitest · edge · high priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:3,quantity:1}], {coupon:'SAVE5'})

**Expected:** Total 4.99 (goods $0 plus shipping).

**Test data:** 1 × $3, SAVE5

**Rationale:** Floor at zero.

### TC6: refuses an unknown coupon code with an error

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC2

**Expected:** Throws an Error whose message contains 'SAVE10'.

**Test data:** coupon SAVE10

**Rationale:** Refusal case for the coupon input.

## Environment

Pure unit tests; no services. Run with `npm test` (vitest run).

## Assumptions

- Prices are in dollars as plain numbers rounded to cents by the module; tests assert exact cent values with toBe.
- Bulk discount and the $50 threshold are compared against the module's exported constants' documented meaning; tests use literal amounts from the ticket so a constant change is caught.
- An unknown coupon is refused by cartTotal throwing an Error; the test checks the error message contains the submitted code, the part the test controls.

## Open questions

- Should coupon codes be case-insensitive (e.g. 'save5')? The code treats them as case-sensitive; not tested.
- Does SAVE5 count when the cart is empty? The code accepts it and charges $0; tested only that the empty cart costs nothing.
