# Test strategy: CART-12 Bulk discount, coupons and free shipping at checkout

Revision 1 · generated by TestForge

## Summary

cartTotal in src/cart.js prices the cart: bulk 10% off at 10+ items (summed across lines), SAVE5 coupon after bulk discount with a $0 floor, unknown coupons refused, and $4.99 shipping unless discounted goods reach $50 (empty cart free). Unit tests with Vitest on cartTotal prove each rule at its boundaries. Reading the code shows bulkDiscount uses `> BULK_THRESHOLD`, so exactly 10 items gets no discount — a suspected defect against AC1, kept as an expected failure.

## Scope

**In scope**
- cartTotal, bulkDiscount behaviour via cartTotal in src/cart.js

**Out of scope**
- UI/checkout pages (none in the repo)

## Test case detail

### TC1: gives 10% off a cart of exactly 10 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · edge · critical priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:4},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 90 (100 less 10%, free shipping). Code currently returns 100 because it uses > 10: marked it.fails and recorded as defect.

**Test data:** lines 4 and 6 at $10

**Rationale:** Boundary of AC1 and quantity summing across lines

### TC2: gives 10% off a cart of 11 items spread over several lines

*unit · vitest · positive · high priority* — covers AC1

**Steps**
1. cartTotal([{price:10,quantity:5},{price:10,quantity:6}])

**Expected:** 99

**Rationale:** A passing multi-line bulk test so a regression in summing quantities is caught (TC1 is expected-fail)

### TC3: gives no bulk discount to a cart of 9 items

*unit · vitest · negative · high priority* — covers AC1

**Expected:** 90 (no discount, free shipping)

**Rationale:** Below threshold

### TC4: takes SAVE5 off after the bulk discount

*unit · vitest · positive · critical priority* — covers AC2

**Steps**

**Expected:** 94 (110 - 11 - 5); coupon-first would give 94.5

**Rationale:** Order of discounts

### TC5: never prices the goods below $0 with SAVE5
